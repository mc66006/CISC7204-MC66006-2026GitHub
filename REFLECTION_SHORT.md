# Checklist reflection (approximately 100 words)

This project demonstrates data cleaning, visualization, Python scripting, and responsible interpretation. I analyzed City of Seattle census-tract apartment rent data from 2001–2025 to examine mixed-income apartment counts, rent levels, and annual rent change. After validation, 3,931 tract-year records remained. Mixed-income count correlated moderately with rent per unit (r = 0.464) and almost not at all with annual rent change (r = -0.014). A random-split regression produced R² = 0.422 and RMSE = $329.08. These are associations, not causal effects; repeated tracts and omitted factors limit conclusions. My next step is time-aware modeling with inflation-adjusted rent, then a peer review and documented improvement.
